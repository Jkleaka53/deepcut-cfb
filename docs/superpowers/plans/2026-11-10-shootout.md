# Shootout of the Week Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Each week the site picks the game most likely to be pure offense, with both teams scoring a lot. It shows the pick as a smaller card under Game of the Week, with a one-line reason in words. A few other high-ranked games get a "Shootout watch" chip.

**Architecture:** A new `cfbm/shootout.py` fits a small ridge regression of points scored by one team, using that offense against that defense on pregame season-to-date stats: points added per play, success rate, explosiveness and pace (plays per game). It's trained on 2015-2025 (minus 2020) from the `/stats/game/advanced` data already cached. A game's shootout score is the **weaker** side's predicted points, so both offenses must qualify. A leave-one-season-out backtest gates the web work. The export writes `shootout.json` per season (pick + reason only) and a `shootoutWatch` flag on game summaries. Predicted points never leave the engine.

**Tech Stack:** Python (uv, pandas, numpy, pytest), Next.js 15 static export, React 19, Vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-edge-score-shootout-upset-design.md`, "Slice 3: Shootout of the Week" and "Decisions from the builder check" 2, 4, 5.

## Global Constraints

- No predicted total, points, margin or over/under in any published file or on the page. Just a pick and a reason in words (spec, anti-goals). `model.json` stays unpublished.
- Game of the Week stays the big star card. Shootout of the Week sits under it as a **smaller** card (owner).
- If the Shootout pick is also the Game of the Week, take the next-best game. If nothing qualifies, hide the card (spec decision 5).
- HARD STOP after Task 3: the backtest numbers go to the owner before any web work. Pass bar (spec decision 4):
  - the weekly pick lands in that week's top 3 combined points more often than `mean(3 / games that week)`
  - West Virginia at Texas Tech, 2026 week 10 (401856856, final 48-53), ranks in its week's top 5
- No new CFBD calls: `offense.plays`, `ppa`, `successRate`, `explosiveness` (and the `defense.*` equivalents) are already in the cached `/stats/game/advanced`.
- Stats strictly before kickoff, FBS vs FBS, both teams with at least `MIN_PRIOR = 3` prior FBS games (same rules as the swing model).
- No animations, square corners, tokens only, light + dark, works at 375px.

## My calls

- **Features** per team-game (CFBD path, offense side and defense side): `ppa` → `offense.ppa`, `sr` → `offense.successRate`, `expl` → `offense.explosiveness`, `plays` → `offense.plays`.
- **Side features** (offense O against defense D, pregame means): `x_ppa = O.off_ppa + D.def_ppa`, `x_sr = O.off_sr + D.def_sr`, `x_expl = O.off_expl + D.def_expl`, `x_pace = O.off_plays + D.def_plays`, plus `home` (1 if O is the home team at a non-neutral site, else 0). Target: O's points in that game.
- **Fit:** standardize the features, then ridge with `l2 = 1.0` via `numpy.linalg.solve`, no penalty on the intercept. Store `means`, `sds`, `coef` and `intercept`, internal only.
- **Score:** `shootout_score(game) = min(pred(away O vs home D), pred(home O vs away D))`. Ties don't matter.
- **Pick per week:** the highest score among qualifying games; skip the Game of the Week id and take the next one. Same week key as GOTW (`"post"` for postseason, else the week number as a string).
- **Shootout watch:** the next 3 highest-scoring qualifying games that week (excluding the pick and the GOTW) get `shootoutWatch: true` on their summary; every other game gets `false`.
- **The reason** (the exact template; ranks among FBS teams in this pregame table, offense ranked by `off_ppa` high-to-low, defense by `def_ppa` low-to-high so 1 = best defense, pace by `off_plays` high-to-low; `n` = teams ranked):
  `"Both offenses score efficiently and both defenses give it up: offenses rank {ao} and {ho} in points added per play, defenses {ad} and {hd} of {n}. Pace: {ap} and {hp} in plays per game."` Ranks are written with `ordinal()` (1st, 22nd). Away team first, then home.
- **Backtest also reports** a "sum" variant (rank by the predicted total) next to the "min" variant, so the owner can see the trade-off. Ship "min" unless the owner picks otherwise at the HARD STOP.
- **Web card:** `PickCard({ tag, game, why, teams })` is a compact card (team logos 24px, names, kickoff, reason) reusable for Upset Watch in slice 4. Tag text: "Shootout of the Week". It sits directly under the GOTW card and follows the same filter rules as `gotwFor`.
- **Chip:** "Shootout watch" as a plain `badge-plain` chip on the home row when `shootoutWatch` is true.

## Review Focus

1. **A week where the best shootout game is the Game of the Week:** the card shows the next-best game, never the same game twice. → Task 4 test `test_pick_skips_gotw`.
2. **Early-season weeks** (no game has two teams with 3+ prior FBS games): no pick for that week, no card, no crash. → Task 4 test `test_no_pick_when_nothing_qualifies`; Task 5 test `card hidden without a pick`.
3. **No predicted points anywhere** in `shootout.json`, the games summaries or the matchups. → Task 4 test `test_shootout_exports_no_numbers`.
4. **A team with NaN pace** (plays missing in a game): the mean skips it. If a side feature is NaN after that, the game doesn't qualify instead of scoring as 0. → Task 2 test `test_nan_feature_disqualifies`.
5. **A filter that hides the pick** (My Teams / conference): the card hides, like GOTW. → Task 5 test `card respects the filter`.
